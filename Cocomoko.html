Tips

  • Place the camera at eye level, about 40â€“70 cm away, so your whole hand fits in the preview.
  • Light your hand from the front; avoid a bright window behind you.
  • Keep the hand inside the preview, palm facing the camera, on a plain background.
  • Move smoothly; pause a moment between gestures.
  • A green skeleton in the preview means the hand is detected; the bar under it names the current gesture.
